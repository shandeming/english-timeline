# English Timeline privacy policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

English Timeline lets you bookmark moments in YouTube videos and replay them for listening practice.

## Data used and stored

The extension reads the current YouTube video ID, playback time, and duration to display and replay bookmarks. It saves video IDs, bookmark timestamps, randomly generated bookmark identifiers, your chosen shortcut, and your replay lead-in setting in your browser profile using chrome.storage.local.

This information stays in your browser. The extension does not transmit it to the developer or third parties and does not use cloud synchronization. It does not collect account details, payment information, authentication information, or video content. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or remotely hosted executable code.

## Permissions and use

The storage permission saves bookmarks and settings between visits and browser restarts. The content script runs only on https://www.youtube.com/* to add controls to the player and respond to your bookmark shortcut. It does not access other websites or your browser's history database.

The extension does not sell, share, or transfer your data. Local data is used only for bookmarking and replay, never for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

## Retention and deletion

Delete individual bookmarks using the × button in the panel. Data remains in the browser profile until you delete it or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes local extension storage. This version provides no export or cloud backup.

## Third parties and contact

YouTube operates independently and has its own privacy practices. This policy describes only English Timeline, an independent project not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change. Questions can be submitted through https://github.com/shandeming/english-timeline/issues.
