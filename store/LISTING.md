## Description to paste

Save the moments you want to hear again without interrupting a YouTube video.

English Timeline adds bookmarks directly above YouTube's progress bar. Press B while listening to mark a moment, then click its mint-colored tick to replay. Use it for English listening practice, lectures, interviews, or any recorded video you want to revisit.

- Mark the current playback time with a keyboard shortcut or the panel's Mark button.
- Click a timeline marker or saved timestamp to replay.
- Replay begins 3 seconds before your mark by default. Adjust the lead-in from 0 to 30 seconds.
- Choose a single letter or digit as your bookmark shortcut.
- Open the star button in the player controls to browse and delete bookmarks.
- Keep separate bookmarks for each video, saved locally between browser visits.
- Use the controls in standard, theater, and fullscreen playback.

To get started, open a recorded YouTube video on a standard watch page and press B. Click the star in the playback controls to see your marks and settings. Shortcuts are ignored while typing or holding modifier keys. Your chosen shortcut takes priority over YouTube's shortcut for that key.

Bookmarks and settings stay in your browser. No extension account, server, analytics, AI, or video download is required.

English Timeline marks the instant you press the key; it does not detect sentence boundaries automatically. This version supports recorded videos on standard YouTube watch pages. Shorts, embedded players, picture-in-picture, ads, and live streams with an indefinite duration are not supported. Uninstalling clears saved bookmarks, and there is no export feature.

English Timeline is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## Privacy practices fields

Single purpose: Allow users to bookmark moments in recorded YouTube videos and replay those moments for listening practice.

Storage justification: Save per-video bookmark timestamps and the user's shortcut and lead-in settings in chrome.storage.local so they persist across refreshes and browser restarts. The extension does not transmit this data.

YouTube site access justification (if requested): The content script runs only on https://www.youtube.com/* to detect the current video and playback time, add bookmark markers and a settings panel to the player, and seek to a saved timestamp. Access across YouTube pages supports its single-page navigation into watch pages.

Remote code: No. All executable JavaScript and CSS are bundled in the extension package.

Data use: The developer receives no user data. Video IDs, bookmark timestamps, and preferences are processed and stored only on the user's device.
